Asclepius

RESEARCH STUDY · REVISION 2.0

← Hermetic texts

THEORETICAL AND TEXTUAL FOUNDATION

The Latin Asclepius preserves a Hermetic dialogue with distinctive discussions of humanity, gods and cult images.

SOURCE ANALYSIS / RESEARCH METHOD

Compare its statue passages with the Coptic parallel where available; distinguish textual witnesses.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT AND INTERPRETATION

The Asclepius discusses humanity's intermediate status and the making of cult images, alongside a lament over Egypt's religious future. Its surviving complete form is Latin.

The image passages, especially sections 23–24, require attention to the dialogue's theological argument. They are not sufficient evidence for reconstructing an Egyptian temple consecration procedure.

REFERENCES & FURTHER READING

Brian Copenhaver, Hermetica (1992); Nock and Festugière, Corpus Hermeticum; Litwa, Hermetica II
