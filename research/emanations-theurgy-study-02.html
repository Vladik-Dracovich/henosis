Demotic magical texts

RESEARCH STUDY · REVISION 2.0

← Late antique theurgy and ritual

THEORETICAL AND TEXTUAL FOUNDATION

Demotic ritual papyri preserve Egyptian-language material alongside cross-cultural practices.

SOURCE ANALYSIS / RESEARCH METHOD

Compare a Demotic witness with Greek parallels only where textual evidence supports the relationship.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT AND INTERPRETATION

Demotic magical papyri preserve Egyptian-language ritual practices alongside Greek and other linguistic material. The London-Leiden papyrus is a particularly important witness.

Language choice, script and ritual vocabulary can reveal contact between traditions, but bilingual material does not establish that all practices belonged to a unified theology. Consult editions of the Demotic texts independently.

REFERENCES & FURTHER READING

Hans Dieter Betz, The Greek Magical Papyri in Translation; Preisendanz, Papyri Graecae Magicae; Iamblichus, De Mysteriis (Clarke, Dillon, Hershbell)
