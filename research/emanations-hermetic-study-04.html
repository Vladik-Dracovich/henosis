Rebirth

RESEARCH STUDY · REVISION 2.0

← Hermetic texts

THEORETICAL AND TEXTUAL FOUNDATION

CH XIII describes rebirth through a dialogue and transformation of the subject.

SOURCE ANALYSIS / RESEARCH METHOD

Analyze the sequence of powers and passions without asserting a recoverable universal initiation rite.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT AND INTERPRETATION

Corpus Hermeticum XIII stages a dialogue between Hermes and Tat concerning rebirth, in which divine powers displace a series of afflictive dispositions.

The language of generation describes a transformation not reducible to biological birth. Read the concluding praise as part of the dialogue's literary movement rather than extracting isolated formulas as a verified ritual script.

REFERENCES & FURTHER READING

Brian Copenhaver, Hermetica (1992); Nock and Festugière, Corpus Hermeticum; Litwa, Hermetica II
