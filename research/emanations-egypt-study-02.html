Coffin Texts

RESEARCH STUDY · REVISION 2.0

← Egyptian textual traditions

THEORETICAL AND TEXTUAL FOUNDATION

Middle Kingdom coffin inscriptions preserve diverse mortuary spells, including cosmographic material and transformations.

SOURCE ANALYSIS / RESEARCH METHOD

Compare spell witnesses in Faulkner and distinguish coffin-specific variants from later Book of the Dead parallels.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT AND INTERPRETATION

The Book of Two Ways, preserved on certain coffins from Deir el-Bersha, maps routes through a dangerous otherworld. Its paths and barriers combine spatial knowledge with verbal protection.

The shift from pyramid walls to coffins changes the relation between text and body. Avoid the older simplistic narrative of a sudden democratization of the afterlife; access to inscribed equipment remained socially differentiated.

REFERENCES & FURTHER READING

Allen, The Ancient Egyptian Pyramid Texts; Faulkner, The Ancient Egyptian Coffin Texts; Hornung, The Ancient Egyptian Books of the Afterlife; TLA — https://thesaurus-linguae-aegyptiae.de/
