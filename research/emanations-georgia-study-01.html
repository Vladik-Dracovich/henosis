Asomtavruli script

RESEARCH STUDY · REVISION 2.0

← Georgian textual traditions

THEORETICAL AND TEXTUAL FOUNDATION

Asomtavruli is an early Georgian script used in inscriptions and manuscripts; script form does not establish language date.

SOURCE ANALYSIS / RESEARCH METHOD

Compare dated inscriptions and manuscript hands; distinguish modern Georgian rendered in old letters from Old Georgian prose.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT AND INTERPRETATION

Asomtavruli appears in early Georgian Christian inscriptions and manuscripts, including monumental writing. Its letter forms belong to the history of Georgian literacy rather than an independent sacred language.

Distinguish a diplomatic transcription from a normalized edition and from a modern text rendered in historical letters. The latter may be visually appropriate for original literature but does not constitute Old Georgian.

REFERENCES & FURTHER READING

Donald Rayfield, The Literature of Georgia; Stephen Rapp, Studies in Medieval Georgian Historiography; National Centre of Manuscripts — https://manuscript.ge/
