RESEARCH STUDY · REVISION 2.0

← Ancient Arabian inscriptions

THEORETICAL AND TEXTUAL FOUNDATION

Damaged inscriptions require explicit editorial conventions for missing and uncertain letters.

SOURCE ANALYSIS / RESEARCH METHOD

Record corpus identifier, photograph, reading and translation; mark supplied letters and avoid translating uncertain restorations as fact.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT AND INTERPRETATION

Epigraphic editions use conventions to distinguish visible letters, damaged traces, restorations and uncertain readings. A photograph or drawing may preserve evidence not obvious in a normalized transcription.

Record corpus identifier, findspot, language, edition and proposed dating. A reconstructed devotional passage should never be presented as an ancient inscription unless its wording is actually attested.
