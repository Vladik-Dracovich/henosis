Greek Magical Papyri

RESEARCH STUDY · REVISION 2.0

← Late antique theurgy and ritual

THEORETICAL AND TEXTUAL FOUNDATION

The PGM corpus preserves diverse ritual texts in Greek and other linguistic contexts, not one unified religion.

SOURCE ANALYSIS / RESEARCH METHOD

Use Preisendanz and Betz with papyrus and line identifiers; distinguish ritual directions from spoken formulas.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT AND INTERPRETATION

The Greek Magical Papyri preserve heterogeneous ritual recipes, hymns and invocations, chiefly from Roman Egypt. Modern numbering organizes manuscripts that were not originally one book.

Read individual recipes with attention to manuscript context and genre. A prayer embedded in a practical ritual may have a different function from a philosophical hymn, even when both invoke the same deity.

REFERENCES & FURTHER READING

Hans Dieter Betz, The Greek Magical Papyri in Translation; Preisendanz, Papyri Graecae Magicae; Iamblichus, De Mysteriis (Clarke, Dillon, Hershbell)

Editorial status: expanded interpretive reading guide. References are suggested reading; this is not a critical edition or independently verified translation.
