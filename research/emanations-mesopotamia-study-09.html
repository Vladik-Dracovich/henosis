Shurpu

RESEARCH STUDY · REVISION 2.0

← Sumerian and Akkadian texts

THEORETICAL AND TEXTUAL FOUNDATION

The series addresses affliction, oath and transgression through ritual and incantation.

SOURCE ANALYSIS / RESEARCH METHOD

Compare enumerations of possible offenses with Maqlu's different ritual focus; avoid assuming modern diagnostic categories.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT AND INTERPRETATION

Shurpu addresses affliction associated with possible violations, curses and oaths, sometimes without a clearly known offense. Enumerations of wrongdoing and ritual release structure its response to uncertainty.

The series illustrates a different diagnostic framework from Maqlu. Compare their treatment of responsibility and purification without collapsing both into a single exorcistic formula.
