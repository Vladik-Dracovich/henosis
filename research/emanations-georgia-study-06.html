Life of Grigol Khandzteli

RESEARCH STUDY · REVISION 2.0

← Georgian textual traditions

THEORETICAL AND TEXTUAL FOUNDATION

The work describes monastic life and institutions in the Georgian literary tradition.

SOURCE ANALYSIS / RESEARCH METHOD

Study its portrayal of authority and ascetic practice; distinguish composition date from events narrated.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT AND INTERPRETATION

Giorgi Merchule's Life of Grigol Khandzteli describes monastic foundations and religious authority in Tao-Klarjeti. It connects spiritual discipline with institutions, patronage and cultural identity.

Read the work as a hagiographic account of community formation. Its portrayal of ascetic virtue differs in purpose from a philosophical theory of the soul's ascent.

REFERENCES & FURTHER READING

Donald Rayfield, The Literature of Georgia; Stephen Rapp, Studies in Medieval Georgian Historiography; National Centre of Manuscripts — https://manuscript.ge/
