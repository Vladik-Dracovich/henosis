Cosmos and divinity

RESEARCH STUDY · REVISION 2.0

← Hermetic texts

THEORETICAL AND TEXTUAL FOUNDATION

Hermetic treatises vary in their treatment of the cosmos as divine, living or a condition of human limitation.

SOURCE ANALYSIS / RESEARCH METHOD

Compare CH IX and XI; preserve tensions rather than harmonizing every statement.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT AND INTERPRETATION

Hermetic descriptions of the cosmos can affirm its beauty and divine order while also presenting embodiment as a condition of limitation. These emphases need not be flattened into a single doctrine.

Compare CH V on the manifestation of the invisible through visible things with CH I on descent. The tension is productive for a comparative theology of manifestation and return.

REFERENCES & FURTHER READING

Brian Copenhaver, Hermetica (1992); Nock and Festugière, Corpus Hermeticum; Litwa, Hermetica II
