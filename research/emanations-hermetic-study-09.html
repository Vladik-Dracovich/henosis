THEORETICAL AND TEXTUAL FOUNDATION

Hermetic prayers connect knowledge, gratitude and divine orientation.

SOURCE ANALYSIS / RESEARCH METHOD

Compare the Prayer of Thanksgiving in its different witnesses and identify translation differences.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT AND INTERPRETATION

The Prayer of Thanksgiving survives in more than one linguistic and textual setting, including the Nag Hammadi collection and the Asclepius tradition. Gratitude is connected with knowledge of the divine.

Compare the prayer's communal voice with the praise concluding CH XIII. The movement from instruction to thanksgiving reveals a literary and devotional pattern without proving that both texts belonged to the same ceremony.
