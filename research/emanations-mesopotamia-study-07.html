Atrahasis

RESEARCH STUDY · REVISION 2.0

← Sumerian and Akkadian texts

THEORETICAL AND TEXTUAL FOUNDATION

The epic relates human creation to divine labor and explores population, catastrophe and survival.

SOURCE ANALYSIS / RESEARCH METHOD

Compare the creation episode with flood traditions; record differences rather than harmonizing them into one account.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT AND INTERPRETATION

Atrahasis begins with divine labor and rebellion, followed by the creation of humans to assume work. The flood narrative emerges from tensions between human multiplication and divine decisions.

The ending introduces measures regulating human life rather than simply restoring the initial condition. Compare the flood with the Gilgamesh epic while preserving the distinctive labor and population themes of Atrahasis.
