Hadramitic traditions

RESEARCH STUDY · REVISION 2.0

← Ancient Arabian inscriptions

THEORETICAL AND TEXTUAL FOUNDATION

Hadramitic inscriptions include dedications to Sayin and evidence of regional cult institutions.

SOURCE ANALYSIS / RESEARCH METHOD

Compare sanctuary provenance and formulaic language; avoid automatic identification with Mesopotamian Sin.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT AND INTERPRETATION

Sayin is prominently attested in Hadramitic religious material, including inscriptions connected with the kingdom's cultic centers. Regional evidence complicates a generalized South Arabian pantheon.

Compare Hadramitic formulas with neighboring corpora without automatically transferring Sabaic divine titles. The local language and sanctuary context are part of the evidence.
