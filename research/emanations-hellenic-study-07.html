Iamblichus and theurgy

RESEARCH STUDY · REVISION 2.0

← Hellenic and Orphic traditions

THEORETICAL AND TEXTUAL FOUNDATION

Iamblichus argues that divine action and ritual symbols cannot be reduced to human intellectual control.

SOURCE ANALYSIS / RESEARCH METHOD

Read De Mysteriis II in context and distinguish his argument from modern ritual invention.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT AND INTERPRETATION

In De Mysteriis II.11, Iamblichus grounds the efficacy of theurgic acts in divine symbols and divine agency rather than human reasoning alone.

This is a philosophical defense of ritual against objections associated with Porphyry. It does not establish that a modern ritual has supernatural effects, nor does it provide a complete handbook of historical ceremonies.

REFERENCES & FURTHER READING

Plotinus, Enneads (Armstrong); Proclus, Elements of Theology (Dodds); Iamblichus, De Mysteriis (Clarke, Dillon, Hershbell); Edmonds, The Orphic Gold Tablets and Greek Religion
