THEORETICAL AND TEXTUAL FOUNDATION

Procession and return provide a useful comparative framework but are not universal ancient categories.

SOURCE ANALYSIS / RESEARCH METHOD

Map each proposed stage to an explicit source and mark gaps; do not infer historical transmission from resemblance alone.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT AND INTERPRETATION

Remaining, procession and return are especially developed in Proclean metaphysics. Other traditions narrate descent, renewal or divine manifestation without necessarily sharing that conceptual scheme.

Compare specific functions: the solar journey renews cosmic order, while Inana's descent concerns death, authority and substitution. Analogies are useful only when the differences remain visible.
