Symbols and tokens

RESEARCH STUDY · REVISION 2.0

← Chaldean Oracles

THEORETICAL AND TEXTUAL FOUNDATION

Later theurgic theory assigns importance to divine symbols or tokens not reducible to ordinary conceptual reasoning.

SOURCE ANALYSIS / RESEARCH METHOD

Compare Iamblichus's discussion with Chaldean citations and distinguish terminology across authors.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT AND INTERPRETATION

The terms symbolon and synthema acquire technical significance in later theurgy, where divine tokens are understood as belonging to a causal order beyond human invention.

Their significance cannot be established by visual resemblance alone. Compare the role of tokens in Iamblichus with their reception in Proclus before interpreting a modern seal as an ancient symbol.

REFERENCES & FURTHER READING

Ruth Majercik, The Chaldean Oracles: Text, Translation, and Commentary (1989); Édouard des Places, Oracles chaldaïques (1971)
