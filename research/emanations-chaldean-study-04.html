Hecate

RESEARCH STUDY · REVISION 2.0

← Chaldean Oracles

THEORETICAL AND TEXTUAL FOUNDATION

Hecate occupies a significant mediating role in Chaldean material and its later interpretations.

SOURCE ANALYSIS / RESEARCH METHOD

Distinguish Chaldean cosmological Hecate from cultic and magical portrayals in other genres.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT AND INTERPRETATION

Hecate is associated with mediation and life-giving power in the Chaldean tradition. Her role cannot be inferred solely from earlier Greek cult titles or later magical representations.

Compare the evidence for Hecate's cosmological position with the reception of the goddess in philosophical commentary. The same divine name may operate differently in a hymn, an oracle and a ritual handbook.

REFERENCES & FURTHER READING

Ruth Majercik, The Chaldean Oracles: Text, Translation, and Commentary (1989); Édouard des Places, Oracles chaldaïques (1971)
