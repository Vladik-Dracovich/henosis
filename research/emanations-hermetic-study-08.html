Kore Kosmou

RESEARCH STUDY · REVISION 2.0

← Hermetic texts

THEORETICAL AND TEXTUAL FOUNDATION

This Hermetic material preserved by Stobaeus develops cosmological and anthropological narratives.

SOURCE ANALYSIS / RESEARCH METHOD

Study its account of soul and embodiment as a distinct textual tradition, not a missing chapter of CH I.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT AND INTERPRETATION

The Kore Kosmou, preserved among the Stobaean Hermetica, presents cosmological teaching through Isis and Horus and develops a narrative concerning souls and embodiment.

Its mythic presentation differs from the revelatory voice of Poimandres. A comparative reading can examine divine instruction and the origin of embodied existence without assuming identical narrative details.

REFERENCES & FURTHER READING

Brian Copenhaver, Hermetica (1992); Nock and Festugière, Corpus Hermeticum; Litwa, Hermetica II
