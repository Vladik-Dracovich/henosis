Technical Hermetica

RESEARCH STUDY · REVISION 2.0

← Hermetic texts

THEORETICAL AND TEXTUAL FOUNDATION

Astrological, alchemical and magical texts attributed to Hermes differ in genre from philosophical dialogues.

SOURCE ANALYSIS / RESEARCH METHOD

Catalogue authorship claims, dates and transmission separately; shared attribution does not establish a single doctrine.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT AND INTERPRETATION

Technical Hermetica include works on astrology, alchemy and ritual knowledge transmitted under the authority of Hermes. Their purposes and textual histories differ from philosophical dialogues.

Attribution to Hermes establishes a claim to authority, not common authorship. Study technical terminology and manuscript transmission separately before integrating symbolic alchemy into a modern metaphysical synthesis.

REFERENCES & FURTHER READING

Brian Copenhaver, Hermetica (1992); Nock and Festugière, Corpus Hermeticum; Litwa, Hermetica II

Editorial status: expanded interpretive reading guide. References are suggested reading; this is not a critical edition or independently verified translation.
