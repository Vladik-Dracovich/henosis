Athtar

RESEARCH STUDY · REVISION 2.0

← Ancient Arabian inscriptions

THEORETICAL AND TEXTUAL FOUNDATION

Athtar appears across South Arabian traditions with varying epithets and cultic associations.

SOURCE ANALYSIS / RESEARCH METHOD

Compare occurrences by language and region; avoid imposing a uniform Venus theology on every dedication.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT AND INTERPRETATION

Athtar is attested across several South Arabian kingdoms and can appear with locally differentiated epithets. His cult is not exhausted by a single astronomical identification.

Compare dedications across linguistic and regional corpora, keeping the epithet attached to its witness. A modern invocation may synthesize these titles only if it identifies the synthesis as new.
