Ritual purification

RESEARCH STUDY · REVISION 2.0

← Late antique theurgy and ritual

THEORETICAL AND TEXTUAL FOUNDATION

Purification instructions vary by genre, location and purpose, and may involve bodily or material preparation.

SOURCE ANALYSIS / RESEARCH METHOD

Compare prescriptions in distinct texts and separate historical description from contemporary practice.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT AND INTERPRETATION

Ancient purification practices include washing, restrictions and preparation of ritual spaces, with requirements varying by text and cult. Purity can concern access to sacred activity rather than moral guilt alone.

Compare the stated purpose and duration of each preparation. A modern symbolic purification may be adapted safely without claiming that it reproduces every ancient material requirement or guarantees divine contact.

REFERENCES & FURTHER READING

Hans Dieter Betz, The Greek Magical Papyri in Translation; Preisendanz, Papyri Graecae Magicae; Iamblichus, De Mysteriis (Clarke, Dillon, Hershbell)
