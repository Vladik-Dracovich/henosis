Divine names and voces magicae

RESEARCH STUDY · REVISION 2.0

← Late antique theurgy and ritual

THEORETICAL AND TEXTUAL FOUNDATION

Ritual texts include names and sound sequences whose linguistic origins may be uncertain.

SOURCE ANALYSIS / RESEARCH METHOD

Transcribe the manuscript reading before proposing etymology; do not assign every sequence a definitive translation.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT AND INTERPRETATION

Voces magicae include sequences that may preserve divine names, foreign words, altered forms or nonlexical sounds. Their interpretation is often uncertain.

Do not manufacture translations from phonetic resemblance. Record the transmitted spelling and variants, and distinguish a proposed etymology from a secure identification before using the sequence in modern composition.

REFERENCES & FURTHER READING

Hans Dieter Betz, The Greek Magical Papyri in Translation; Preisendanz, Papyri Graecae Magicae; Iamblichus, De Mysteriis (Clarke, Dillon, Hershbell)
