THEORETICAL AND TEXTUAL FOUNDATION

A contemporary theurgical composition can draw on ancient sources while remaining a new work.

SOURCE ANALYSIS / RESEARCH METHOD

Label quotations, adaptations and original passages individually; document language review and avoid claiming historical authenticity for reconstructed sequences.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT AND INTERPRETATION

A modern theurgical text can combine original prayers with documented ancient passages, but the two must be typographically and editorially distinct. The source of every borrowed passage should be traceable.

Use a critical edition, record passage numbers and identify the translator or provide a clearly labeled new translation. Where the ancient language cannot be verified, retain a modern-language composition rather than inventing an authoritative ancient formula.

REFERENCES & FURTHER READING

Hans Dieter Betz, The Greek Magical Papyri in Translation; Preisendanz, Papyri Graecae Magicae; Iamblichus, De Mysteriis (Clarke, Dillon, Hershbell)
