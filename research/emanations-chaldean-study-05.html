Fire imagery

RESEARCH STUDY · REVISION 2.0

← Chaldean Oracles

THEORETICAL AND TEXTUAL FOUNDATION

Fire functions as a powerful image of divine activity and ascent in the surviving material.

SOURCE ANALYSIS / RESEARCH METHOD

Catalogue literal and metaphorical uses and identify the source of each quotation before synthesizing them.

HISTORICAL CONTEXT AND INTERPRETATION

Fiery imagery expresses divine luminosity, activity and transcendence. The language of fire can describe an intelligible reality without implying that it is ordinary combustion.

Distinguish metaphor, ritual image and metaphysical predicate. A modern visualization of divine fire is an adaptation, not a recovered physical technique or a scientific account of energy.

REFERENCES & FURTHER READING

Ruth Majercik, The Chaldean Oracles: Text, Translation, and Commentary (1989); Édouard des Places, Oracles chaldaïques (1971)
