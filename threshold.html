I · THE PRIMORDIAL HEAVENS

In those distant days, when destinies were decreed, An brought abundance into being. Heaven and earth stood beneath the authority of the divine powers.

II · THE DIVINE ORDER

Enlil, the great mountain, cherished Enki. The divine powers and the crafts of the world were entrusted to the lord of the deep, and the lands received their appointed order.

III · THE EMERGENCE OF WISDOM

Enki, king of the Abzu, raised his sacred house amid the shining waters. He was celebrated as the wisdom and understanding of the lands, keeper of the ordinances of heaven and earth.
